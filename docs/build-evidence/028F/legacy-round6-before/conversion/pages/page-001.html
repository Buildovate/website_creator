BEFORE YOU CHOOSE THE WORK

A small leak.
A bigger question.

A drip can leave you wondering how much work your roof really needs. You do not need to figure that out alone. Start with what you’ve noticed, then talk through the details around it.

  1. 01

    Follow where the water travels.

    A ceiling stain may sit away from where water enters. Ask about the flashing, joints and roof covering around the suspected source.

  2. 02

    Look at the roof around it.

    An isolated detail and widespread wear call for different conversations. Ask which nearby materials can stay and what needs closer inspection.

  3. 03

    Know what your repair includes.

    Before agreeing to work, compare the proposed repair area, included materials and how any concealed damage will be handled.

Bring the question, not a diagnosis.Describe the stain or drip from inside your home. There is no need to climb onto the roof to start a conversation.
