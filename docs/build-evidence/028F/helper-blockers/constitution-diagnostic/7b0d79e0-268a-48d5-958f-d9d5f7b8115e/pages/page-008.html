YOUR ROOF, YOUR DECISION

  • Start with the roof itself.

    Agree on the layers, materials, flashing and ventilation, and ask what happens if the crew finds damaged decking.

  • Put the whole job in writing.

    Include the work schedule, cleanup and how changes to the estimate will be discussed with you.

  • Compare the full cost.

    Read the APR, fees, repayment term and total payments together. A smaller monthly payment can mean paying more over time.

  • Choose at your own pace.

    Keep room for the rest of your household budget. Confirm your project agreement and any financing approval before work begins.
