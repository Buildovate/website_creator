A monthly payment
is only part of the cost.

Financing is one way to plan a project. Here’s what to consider before choosing it.

01

Keep room in your budget.

Spreading eligible project costs can leave cash available for other priorities. Choose payments you can comfortably afford.

02

Plan around your timing.

Explore whether financing can help align a needed improvement with your budget. Confirm approval and scheduling separately.

03

See the whole picture.

Compare your monthly payment with total interest and fees. A longer term can lower payments while increasing total cost.

A ROOF PLAN YOU CAN UNDERSTAND

Know what’s in the roof.
Then weigh how to pay.

A written roofing estimate should explain the materials, the work beneath them and what happens if damaged decking is found. Settle those questions before comparing payment options.

BEFORE YOU COMPARE PAYMENTS

  1. 01

    Start with the roof itself.

    Ask which layers will be repaired or replaced, and how flashing and ventilation fit into the scope.

  2. 02

    Leave fewer questions open.

    Discuss cleanup, the work schedule and how changes to the written estimate would be handled.

  3. 03

    Compare more than a payment.

    Read the APR, repayment term and total cost together. Take the time you need to decide.
