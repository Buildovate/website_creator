Homes and conditions in Oxnard.

Oxnard’s coastal neighborhoods see marine air, while the Henry T. Oxnard Historic District includes older homes with details worth keeping. Around Oxnard Shores, Channel Islands and downtown, salt exposure, roof edges and the route rainwater takes deserve a close look.

Planning work in Oxnard.

Does an Oxnard Shores home need different materials?

Near the beach, discuss corrosion resistance for flashing, fasteners and outdoor equipment; the condition of the existing assembly matters as much as its age.

Can you preserve details on an older downtown home?

Bring photos of the eaves, trim and earlier additions so the scope can retain the character of your Oxnard home.

What should I photograph before a coastal roof visit?

From the ground, photograph the affected roof edge, staining and the side facing the ocean; avoid climbing onto a wet surface.

Which Oxnard neighborhoods can I ask about?

Send an address in Oxnard Shores, Channel Islands or the downtown area to discuss access and the work you need.
