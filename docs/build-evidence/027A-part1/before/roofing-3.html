Questions from the coast.

Does living near the ocean decide which roof I should choose?

Exposure is one consideration; the building, existing assembly and material compatibility also belong in the discussion. Ask how the proposed option suits this particular property.

What is useful to share about an older Oxnard house?

Photographs of its eaves and any records of previous changes can help explain the details you want to retain.

Should I go onto the roof to check a stain?

Keep your observations to places you can reach safely. Describe the indoor mark and its timing; leave access to the roof for an appropriate assessment.

Why mention a stain that appears only during winter rain?

Its timing can help frame the inspection. Describe where the mark begins, whether it dries between storms and any changes you have noticed indoors; a stain alone does not establish its source.
