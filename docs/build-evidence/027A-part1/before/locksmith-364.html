04 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Your lock installation questions.

Lock installation / work detail
Can I supply the deadbolt I want installed?

Share its model and door measurements before booking. The installer needs to check the backset, thickness range and existing preparation before confirming the fit.

Why does my door only lock when I lift it?

The hinges, door position or strike alignment may be contributing. Inspecting the opening can avoid replacing a sound lock without addressing the cause.

Does lock installation include repairing the door frame?

Describe any split wood or loose strike screws when requesting a scope in Oxnard. QA Locksmith can separate the hardware work from any frame repairs that are needed.
