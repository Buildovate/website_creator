What information helps when I am locked outside?

Describe the door, the lock type and whether a key is lost, inside or broken. Confirm your address and permission to enter when arranging help.

Will the door need a new lock afterward?

That depends on its condition and the agreed entry method. Ask the technician to explain any proposed replacement before work begins.
