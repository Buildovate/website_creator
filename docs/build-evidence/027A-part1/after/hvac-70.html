Homes and conditions in Ventura.

Ventura has older houses around Midtown and the Westside, coastal homes in Pierpont, and varied hillside rooflines. Marine moisture can linger near the shore; on sloped lots, runoff, equipment access and protecting the downhill side of the property all affect the work plan.
