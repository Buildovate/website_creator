Your key duplication questions.

Key duplication / work detail
Can any key be copied from an existing spare?

Wear and copying errors can carry forward. Bring the best working original available; restricted profiles and some electronic keys require a different authorized service.

Why does a freshly cut copy feel rough?

Blank compatibility, cut accuracy, burrs and the condition of the original can affect fit. Stop forcing a reluctant copy and have it checked against the working key.

Where can I arrange key duplication in Oxnard?
