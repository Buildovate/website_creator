Your smart locks questions.

Smart locks / work detail
Will a keypad still work if Wi-Fi is down?

Offline operation varies by model. Review its local keypad, battery requirements and physical backup before deciding which features matter to your household.

Can I keep my current key as a backup?

Some devices retain an existing cylinder while others replace it. Bring the model details so the lock and key arrangement can be checked together.

Who controls the codes after installation?

The property owner should hold the administrator account and decide who receives access. QA Locksmith can walk through the selected device with you during a smart-lock visit in Oxnard.
