04 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Your commercial access control questions.

Commercial access control / work detail
Can you work with our existing badge readers?

Supply the manufacturer, controller details and available administration records. Compatibility and account access need checking before any hardware or credential changes are proposed.

Can different staff groups have different permissions?

Role and schedule controls depend on the chosen system. List the doors and hours each group needs so those requirements can be compared with supported features.

What should a business prepare for an access-control survey?

Bring an authorized contact, a door list and the current credential process. QA Locksmith can use those details to scope the commercial access control requested in Oxnard.
