OUR SERVICES

01

Residential lockout

Locked outside with the keys indoors? Explain the door and lock so the technician can discuss an appropriate way to help.

Explore residential lockout
02

Rekeying

Moving in or changing who has a key? A compatible cylinder can be reset without replacing every piece of door hardware.

Explore rekeying
03

Lock installation

A new deadbolt or handle needs more than a matching finish. Door thickness, bore spacing and strike alignment determine the fit.

Explore lock installation
04

Smart locks

Keypad entry can simplify coming and going. Start with door compatibility, battery access and a backup way in before choosing connected features.

Explore smart locks
05

Automotive lockout

Keys left in a locked vehicle need help suited to that vehicle. Share the make, model and year before arranging an access visit.

Explore automotive lockout
06

Commercial access control

Manage staff entry around the doors your business uses. Readers, credentials and exit hardware need a coordinated plan, not disconnected devices.

Explore commercial access control
07

Safe servicing

A reluctant handle, worn keypad or forgotten combination calls for a careful assessment of the safe, its condition and the owner access needs.

Explore safe servicing
08

Key duplication

A useful spare starts with the right blank and a sound original. Check the key profile before cutting, then test it in the intended lock.

Explore key duplication
