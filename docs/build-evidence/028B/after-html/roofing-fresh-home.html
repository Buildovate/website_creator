WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

A roof question.
A clearer way forward.

Start with what you’ve noticed. Keep the choices in your hands.

  1. 01

    Your note

    Tell us where you noticed a drip, stain or loose material. A photo from a safe place can help start the conversation.

  2. 02

    A closer look

    Discuss an inspection of the ridge, flashing and gutter around your concern. Ask what is visible and what still needs checking.

  3. 03

    The written scope

    Review the findings, proposed repair area and materials. Keep the work included and any unanswered questions together.

  4. 04

    Your next step

    Talk through the price and timing before choosing how to proceed. Availability and an appointment are confirmed separately.
