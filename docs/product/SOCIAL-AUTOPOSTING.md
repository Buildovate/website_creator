# Metricool social auto-posting — #030M

Status: product specification for a future customer feature. This publishes for each contractor's own connected accounts; it is not Buildovate marketing. All contractor screens follow [UX-RULES.md](UX-RULES.md). Full previews use trade-specific content tagged `Sample` and never schedule it; published automation uses confirmed contractor data only. This spec records planning assumptions and requires a live API/terms review before implementation.

## Engine and launch gates

- Use Metricool's **REST API** as the production scheduling, publishing and analytics engine on an Advanced plan, with one Metricool brand per contractor. Advanced has configurations up to 50 brands; growth beyond 50 requires a Metricool **Custom** quote. Record the Custom price and unit economics as an open cost question in #015G. [Metricool plan comparison](https://help.metricool.com/plans-add-ons-and-api-access-explained-xux1u), [current pricing](https://metricool.com/pricing/).
- **Launch blocker:** obtain written confirmation from Metricool that its Advanced API and terms allow Buildovate to power a contractor-facing product this way, including brand provisioning, client connection, posting, approval, analytics and branding. Do not launch on an inferred agency-use right. Confirm rate limits, scopes and the endpoint contract from Metricool's current API reference before code is built.
- Metricool's official [MCP server](https://ai.metricool.com/mcp) may help internal operations and AI-assisted research, but it is **not** the production posting engine. Keep production API credentials encrypted server-side and scoped to staff; do not expose them to contractor browsers. [MCP help](https://help.metricool.com/how-to-connect-metricools-mcp-eqp9h).

## Brand and account connection

1. Provision one tenant-mapped brand with the contractor's confirmed name and approved logo through the proposed `POST /v2/settings/brands` API call. Store the returned brand ID with tenant isolation; prevent duplicate creation on retries. The exact request/response fields need a live API contract check.
2. Generate the contractor's per-brand account connection link through the proposed `POST /v2/settings/brands/connections/anonymous-link` call. Contractor authorizes the social network directly through Metricool and **never shares a password with Buildovate**. A link lasts **71 hours**; display its expiration time in the contractor/admin view and offer **Send a new link**, invalidating or superseding the old one. Check that the intended accounts connected to the intended brand. [Metricool connection-link guidance](https://help.metricool.com/how-to-share-a-link-so-your-clients-can-connect-their-social-networks-without-a-metricool-account-7ocal).
3. The connection experience displays Metricool branding; do not promise a white-label connect screen or try to conceal its logo on Advanced. Contractor-facing setup copy says “You'll connect securely through Metricool.” Verify exact branding treatment in the live flow before launch.

## Monthly lead-focused content

- Prepare **20 posts per contractor per month** for the full pack, or **12** for the Growth light pack. Adapt format and schedule to each actually connected network; a pack item may have network-specific variants but must not inflate the count by syndicating identical copy. Use the site's trade, selected services, service-area cities and variation seed so contractors do not receive generic shared art or copy.
- Source only permissioned, real projects/photos, real sourced reviews, active approved promotions and financing, actual served services/cities, checked seasonal/trade tips, and prices from the contractor's approved CRM price book. Keep provenance per post. A tip may be educational without pretending a local event or job occurred. No invented ratings, reviews, prices, discounts, outcomes, scarcity or urgency. If evidence is missing, regenerate a different truthful idea or leave the slot unfilled; do not publish a sample.
- Every post has one clear CTA to the contractor's site, [Virtual Estimator #029V](VIRTUAL-ESTIMATOR.md) or tap-to-call. Use a unique UTM network/campaign/post ID on links so page visits, estimator starts and CRM leads can be attributed. Do not claim call attribution without tracked call evidence. If a financing payment appears, show APR and term adjacent to it in the actual network rendering and get K23 approval.
- Suggest a best-time slot per network from Metricool when available; distinguish account-specific data from general guidance and fall back to an editable schedule in the contractor's business time zone when unavailable. [Metricool best-time explanation](https://help.metricool.com/best-time-to-post-on-social-media-in-metricool-w7ll9).

## Approval, scheduling and health

- **Default: approval before posting.** Show the month pack in a mobile-first dashboard. The contractor may approve all eligible posts, or approve, edit, skip or regenerate an individual post. Revalidate claims, image permission, CTA, character/media limits and financing disclosures after every edit or regeneration. A skipped/unanswered post is not queued.
- Auto-approve is an explicit per-category contractor setting with a visible summary and audit history; it never silently applies to categories without permission. Use Metricool approval/roles where the live API supports the workflow; otherwise keep approval in Buildovate and send **only approved** posts to Metricool. A scheduler refuses any pending, skipped, disconnected or expired-source item.
- Poll connection status **daily** because a usable disconnection webhook is not documented. Meta, LinkedIn and Pinterest may need periodic reconnects around 60 days; treat that interval as an operational planning estimate, not a guaranteed token lifetime. On disconnection, pause that network's queue, alert the contractor with a fresh one-tap connection link, and show the state to admins. Do not block other healthy networks.
- Show failed posts with reason, provider ID, last attempt and a Retry action. Use a stable post ID/idempotency record and reconcile provider state before retry; never double-post when a response is uncertain. Keep a durable audit trail of approval, schedule, publish, retry and reconnect events.

## Reporting and commercial model

- Monthly per-contractor report: real posts published by network, available reach and engagement from Metricool, link clicks, estimator starts and CRM leads attributed by UTM/post. Explain attribution window and avoid assigning unmatched leads. Hide a metric with no data rather than showing zero as if measured; distinguish unavailable provider metrics from a measured zero.
- **Planning cost estimate supplied for review, not a booked expense:** Metricool Advanced approximately **$3.18–$6.70 per contractor/month** depending on brand count and billing, plus approximately **$5.11** AI-generation cost for a 20-post pack, yielding approximately **$8.30–$11.80** total. Recalculate from current invoices, seat occupancy, token usage and any Custom quote before pricing or margin claims. #015G records actual per-contractor subscription allocation, model/effort usage and X add-on cost; never seed sample costs as real.
- Proposed entitlement: **Growth includes the 12-post light pack** and **All-in includes the full 20-post pack**. Lower plans may opt into the full pack for **$97/month**. Encode these inclusions in #015H's exact plan entitlements before sale; no automatic upgrade, overage or X charge.
