## Money rules

Integer cents, immutable SKU/recipe versions, explicit tax treatment and verified-payment ledger follow Pack 3. Do not treat a manually marked Paid contact or signed draft as settlement. Subscription grant once per paid period; duplicate provider events cannot grant twice. Cancelling an automation does not implicitly cancel billing; expose both controls and renewal date. No silent overages, reward payouts, new provider subscriptions or ad-budget charges.

The actual live timer is 900 seconds. Discount is 10% of eligible add-on lines; base website/fixed website-video bundle excluded. Monthly recurrence: first paid month only. Annual: first paid year of Seasonal Pass/Pro only; other annual products remain excluded. Dedicated $99 Spanish rule is mutually exclusive. Show accepted fee total, renewal and payment-validity deadline; never combine an expired timer with a new offer to manufacture urgency.
