Implementation: a curated library of original component recipes; three compositions, three type treatments, three card treatments and three spacing rhythms. Checkbox locks control randomization. Effects can be selected independently. The engine composes supported CSS and interactions rather than asking a runtime AI model to rewrite source code. No claim of guaranteed global uniqueness. Business content and brand color remain unchanged when applying a composition. New creations receive the selected settings. Publication remains a separate action.

Service artwork: 48 newly generated specialty crops plus existing roofing illustrations mapped to all eight roofing specialties. 56 named service mappings across seven trades; unknown/custom names do not receive unrelated artwork. Seven generated cutaway base images use percentage-aligned SVG annotation layers and accessible HTML buttons. These are conceptual explanatory illustrations, not engineering diagrams or evidence of completed projects.

Review adapters fetch on visitor request and store no provider content. Google overall rating and review count stay unchanged. Four-star-plus filtering applies only to the returned Google review sample and is disclosed. Yelp currently supplies an aggregate and source link, not filtered excerpts. Source links work without runtime keys; live results require approved provider configuration. Review display rules should be rechecked when enabling a provider in production.

Forms now support ten field types, up to twenty custom fields, stable keys, option validation, required groups, numeric bounds and typed server validation. Existing fixed name/email/phone/consent fields remain. Custom forms retain their own URLs; this change does not automatically replace every built-in homepage inquiry form.
